Raw to ready 굽는 법

Four steps around one fire, clockwise. We start you off; after that the tongs are yours.

“We turn the first round with you. After that, nobody touches your grill unless you ask.”

Dae-ho ParkHead butcher, Hwaro
  1. 01Choose

    Pick your cuts at the counter

    Point at the meat, or tell us how hungry you are. Two plates of meat between two people is plenty; we will say so if you over-order.

  2. 02Light

    The coals arrive glowing

    Lump charcoal, lit in the yard for forty minutes and carried to your table in a steel pot. The grill goes on when the coals stop spitting.

  3. 03Grill

    We turn the first round

    A cook lays the first plate, flips it once and cuts it with scissors so you can see how long it wants. Then you take over.

  4. 04Finish

    Fried rice in the last fat

    Rice, kimchi and seaweed pressed onto the hot plate until it crackles. Or a stew set over what is left of the fire.

Where the fire came from 걸어온 길

From one borrowed grill in a car park to fourteen tables on Blossom Street.

  1. 2016

    A grill in a car park

    Saturday nights behind a tyre shop in Ancoats: one grill, two cuts and a queue that went round the block by the third week.

  2. 2018

    Thirty covers on Blossom Street

    A former print shop, six tables with grills dropped into them, and extraction that took a whole winter to get right.

  3. 2021

    The ageing room

    A glass room for whole loins behind the counter, and a butcher who came for a month and stayed.

  4. 2024

    Upstairs and the soju bar

    The floor above became the back room, the old storeroom a bar, and the restaurant 62 covers on a busy night.

Good to know 안내

Anything else, ring the restaurant from 15:00. Someone at the counter always picks up.

Call +44 161 000 0000
Do I need to book?

For a table, yes; we hold it for fifteen minutes. The soju bar and the ageing counter are walk-in only, and there is usually a seat after ten.

Will I go home smelling of smoke?

Less than you think. Every grill has its own hood that pulls the smoke straight up, and we hand out a coat bag at the door. Your hair is another matter.

Someone in our group does not eat meat

Ask for the garden set: king oyster mushrooms, tofu, rice cakes and every banchan, cooked on a grill that never sees meat.

What about allergens?

Soy, sesame, wheat and fish sauce run through most marinades. Tell us when you book and the kitchen will mark every plate that comes to your table.

Can we bring children?

Until eight, yes. We bring a high chair and keep the grill nearest small hands for the cooks to work.

How does a group of twelve work?

The back room seats twenty. Book the long table set and we take a £10 deposit a head, taken off the bill on the night.
