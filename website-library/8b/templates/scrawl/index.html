hello@scrawl.example
When do drops land?

Every other Friday at 6 pm CET, online and in the studio shop at the same minute. The calendar above lists the next five.

How do I send a doodle?

Photograph it in daylight and email it to doodles@scrawl.example before the last Sunday of the month. Paper, napkins and phone sketches all count.

What if my doodle is picked?

You get a fee, ten percent of the print's sales, a box of the finished pieces and your name on every tag.

Shipping and returns

We ship worldwide in recycled mailers. Returns are free within 30 days, unworn and unwashed.

How do the sizes run?

Boxy and a touch long. Take your usual size for the intended fit, one down for a closer one.
