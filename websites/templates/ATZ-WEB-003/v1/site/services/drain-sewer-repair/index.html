YOUR SERVICE, EXPLAINED

When drainage problems go beyond a clog

Drain and sewer lines can develop restrictions or damage over time. Service Company lists drain and sewer repair among its services for blocked, damaged or aging lines. The information below is general guidance, not a diagnosis.

02 / PLUMBING INSIGHTS

Patterns help explain the problem

Backups affecting several fixtures, recurring blockages or changes in drainage throughout the home may signal an issue involving a shared line rather than a single drain.
